

# 🎓 GoTrainee

### Connecting Students with Real-World Opportunities

GoTrainee is a university team project designed to connect students and graduates with organizations through practical training opportunities, skill assessments, and project-based learning.

Our goal is to help learners develop practical skills while helping organizations define their needs and discover suitable talent.

---

## 💡 Key Features

- 👤 **User Profiles** — Student and organization profiles.
- 🛠️ **Skill Management** — Skills and proficiency levels.
- 🧩 **Coding Tasks** — Practical programming tasks based on student skills.
- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Evaluation** — AI-supported feedback on coding submissions.
- 🏢 **Organization Requests** — Organizations can describe their project needs.
- 💬 **AI Requirements Intake** — Guided questions to help organizations clarify their requirements.
- 📋 **Project Specifications** — Structured project requirements for training opportunities.

> GoTrainee is under development. Features are being implemented as part of our university project.
